DATA ARCHITECTURE & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

FishIn: Fishing Log & Spots is built by an independent developer in North Macedonia. We believe your private fishing spots belong strictly to you. Here is an honest, technical breakdown of what data exists, where it is stored, and how it is protected. This policy covers the FishIn Android app and fishinapp.site. The developer is the data controller; contact: contact@fishinapp.site.

VERSION 3.0 • LAST UPDATED: 24 OCTOBER 2026 • ENCRYPTED HTTPS

What Stays on Your Device

CLIENT EDGE

Private data generated in the field is retained strictly on your phone by default:

  • Private Spots & Waypoint Notes: Coordinates and personal annotations remain in local storage unless you explicitly choose to publish them.
  • Local Catch Photos: Media captured in standard mode is stored in device storage unless Pro Cloud Backup is toggled.
  • High-Precision GPS Location: Position data is queried exclusively on your device to orient the offline vector map and calculate nearest water bodies; it is never tracked or logged in the background.

Catch Log & Tackle Box Sync

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

Telemetry required to restore your angler record across devices:

  • Catch Metadata: Species taxonomy, weight, length, timestamps, lure selection, water conditions, and notes are synced to your private account record.
  • Confidentiality: Catch logs are strictly private. Other anglers can never view your catches unless you actively share a post to the community feed.
  • FishIn Pro Cloud Photo Backup: With Pro, compressed catch and lure photos are archived to our Frankfurt server. If Pro expires, existing backups are held for 90 days before permanent deletion.
  • Sign-Out Behavior: Signing out wipes local synced databases from the handset; authenticating rehydrates your full history.

External Services & AI Processing

ON REQUEST ONLY

Live integrations invoked strictly to deliver real-time features:

  • Atmospheric & Solunar Data: Coordinates of active spot queries are transmitted to Open-Meteo to fetch pressure, wind, and water variables.
  • Google Maps & Satellite Tiles: Map viewports load vector and satellite tiles from Google. Device IP and viewport bounds are processed according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • FishIn Pro Multimodal AI: Tactical weather briefs, lure recommendations, and tackle photo scans are sent through our server to Google's Gemini API, which processes them under Google's API terms and may store them for a limited time for abuse monitoring. We keep a daily count of AI requests per account to manage quotas. We do not use your data to train AI models. Do not include personal information in photos or questions you send to AI features.

Identity & Community Sharing

YOU CHOOSE

Account isolation and social visibility controls:

  • Anonymous by Default: Every fresh installation is provisioned an anonymous random UUID so offline and local features function instantly without mandatory signup.
  • Google Sign-In: Authenticating links your verified name, email address, and avatar. You select a public username; personal bio and public username are visible to other anglers.
  • Selective Spot Sharing: Shared spots disclose name and public pin coordinates only. Your private notes and honeypot notes are strictly excluded.
  • Shared Catch Privacy: Shared catches display species, weight/length, lure, and an optional macro spot region name. Exact GPS coordinates are never disclosed.
  • Community Ratings: Spot star ratings and review metrics are aggregated into anonymous score averages; individual review texts of private spots remain on your phone.

Purchases & Subscriptions

PLAY BILLING

FishIn Pro subscriptions are processed exclusively via Google Play:

  • Zero Financial Data Access: We never receive, process, or store credit card numbers, billing addresses, or bank details.
  • Purchase Token Validation: Our API verifies Google Play purchase tokens, SKU product IDs, and expiration timestamps to grant Pro entitlements.

Data Storage & Infrastructure

EU FRANKFURT

Infrastructure located in the European Union:

  • Database Tier: User records, logs, and social metadata are stored in our own PostgreSQL database on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • API & Photo Vault: Sign-in, the core API and all photos (profile pictures, shared catches and Pro backups) are hosted on the same Oracle Cloud server in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Sign-in Emails: One-time sign-in codes are sent through Google's Gmail.
  • Server Logs: Like any web service, our server briefly keeps technical logs (IP address, time, request path, app version) to keep FishIn secure and working. They are deleted automatically.
  • Transfers Outside the EU: Google (Maps, Gemini, Play, Gmail) may process data in other countries, including the United States, under its own safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • No Tracking & No Ads: FishIn does not sell personal data, does not include third-party advertising SDKs, and does not run invasive tracking networks.

Retention & Deletion Lifecycle

AUTO-PURGE

Strict data disposal and customer sovereignty policies:

  • One-Tap Account Deletion: Accessible in-app at Settings › Account › Delete account. Deletes profile, catch logs, tackle box, photo archives, posts, shared spots, and friends. See Account Deletion Guide.
  • Dormant Account Purge: Unused anonymous accounts without saved records are permanently purged after 90 days of inactivity.
  • Local Handset Purge: Uninstalling the Android application instantly wipes all on-device caches, tile databases, and local notes.
  • Backups: Deleted data may remain in server backups for up to 30 days before it is overwritten.
  • Legal Holds: We may keep limited data longer where the law requires it, or to deal with abuse reports, fraud or legal claims.

Security & User Rights

YOUR RIGHTS

Access controls, statutory compliance, and contact options:

  • Encryption in Transit: All traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS.
  • Access Controls: The database only lets each account read its own private records.
  • No Guarantee: We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee that data will never be accessed, lost or disclosed without authorisation. If a breach affects you, we will notify you where the law requires.
  • Legal Bases (GDPR): We process data to provide the service you asked for (contract), to keep it secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interest), and where you choose to share content (consent, which you can withdraw by deleting it).
  • Children's Privacy: FishIn is intended for people aged 13 and older (or the higher age of digital consent in your country, unless a parent or guardian consents). We do not knowingly collect data from younger children; if you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.
  • Your Rights: Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, export, delete or restrict the use of your data, or object to its use. Email contact@fishinapp.site; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Changes: We may update this policy. Material changes will be shown in the app or on this page with a new date.
