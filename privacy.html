Privacy Policy

The short version

  • No ads, no trackers. FishIn has no advertising or analytics SDKs, and we never sell your data.
  • You choose who sees your spots and catches: everyone, your FishIn friends, or only you. Your notes are always private.
  • Your data lives in the EU, on our own server at Oracle Cloud in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Fish Coach chats stay on your phone. Questions are sent to Google Gemini through our server only to answer them.
  • Delete everything in the app under Settings › Account › Delete account.

What stays on your phone

Much of FishIn works on the phone alone, and that data never leaves it unless you share or sync it:

  • Your location. Used on the phone to centre the map, find spots near you and give a forecast for where you are. We don't track your location in the background or keep a history of it.
  • Saved spots and offline countries. Spot data you've viewed or downloaded is cached on the phone so the map works without signal.
  • Fish Coach chats and memory. Your conversations, and the short notes Fish Coach remembers about how you fish, are stored on the phone.
  • Photos you don't back up. Without Pro photo backup, catch and lure photos stay on the phone.

Uninstalling FishIn removes all of this from the phone.

Your account

Every install starts with a guest account: a random ID with no name or email, so you can use FishIn straight away and your log is kept safe.

You can sign in at any time, and everything you saved as a guest comes with you:

  • With Google: we receive your name, email address and profile photo. Nothing else.
  • With email: we send a one-time sign-in code to your address. The code email goes out through Google's Gmail.

If you join the community you pick a public username, and can add a profile photo and short bio that other anglers see.

Sharing and community

  • Spots you share with everyone appear on every angler's map; friends-only spots appear only for your FishIn friends. Shared spots show the name and pin, never your notes.
  • Catches you share show the species, size, lure, photo and the name of the spot's area. They never show exact coordinates.
  • Leaderboard entries come only from catches you share with everyone.
  • You can remove anything you've shared, report content and block other users at any time.

Fish Coach and AI

Fish Coach, AI fishing briefs, lure picks, the lure scanner and fish photos are FishIn Pro features. They run on Google Gemini, always through our own server, which holds the key and builds the request.

To answer, a request may include your question, a photo you send, the spot and its forecast, the fish recorded there, your tackle box, recent messages from that chat and the notes Fish Coach remembers about you. We count each account's AI use against a monthly allowance, with short hourly and daily limits on a few heavier tasks, to keep usage fair. We don't keep your questions, answers or photos on our server, and we don't use your data to train AI models.

Google processes these requests under the Gemini API terms and may store them for a limited time to monitor for abuse. Please don't put personal information in photos or questions you send to AI features.

Like any web service, our server briefly keeps technical logs (IP address, time, request path and app version) to keep FishIn secure and working. They are deleted automatically.

Purchases

FishIn Pro is sold only through Google Play. We never see your card number, billing address or bank details. Our server checks the purchase token Google Play gives the app, to switch Pro on for your account and keep it in step with renewals and cancellations.

Phone permissions

  • Location: to show spots and forecasts near you. FishIn works without it; you can search the map instead.
  • Camera: to photograph catches, lures and fish for Fish Coach. Used only when you take a photo.
  • Photos: only the pictures you pick are read.
  • Internet: to load maps, forecasts, sync and the community.

How long we keep data

  • Your account is kept until you delete it.
  • Guest accounts not opened for 90 days are deleted with everything in them, unless they ever had Pro.
  • Pro photo backups are kept for 90 days after Pro ends, so you can renew or take them with you, and then deleted.
  • Deleting your account removes your profile, username, catch log, tackle box, shared spots, posts, comments, friends and photos straight away. Deletion can't be undone.
  • Backups: deleted data may stay in server backups for up to 30 days before it's overwritten.
  • Legal holds: we may keep limited data longer where the law requires it, or to deal with abuse reports, fraud or legal claims.

See how to delete your account.

Legal bases and transfers

Under the GDPR we process your data:

  • To provide the service you asked for (contract): your account, log, sync, forecasts and Pro.
  • To keep FishIn secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interest): server logs, rate limits and moderation.
  • Where you choose to share (consent): spots, catches and posts you make public or share with friends. You can withdraw it by deleting them.

Our own server is in the EU. Google (Maps, Gemini, Play, Sign-In and Gmail) may process data in other countries, including the United States, under its own safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, restrict how it's used, or object to its use. Much of this you can do yourself in the app.

For anything else, email contact@fishinapp.site. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security and children

All traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS. The database only lets each account read its own private records, and the keys for Gemini, Google Play and email live on the server, never in the app.

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure and we can't guarantee that data will never be accessed, lost or disclosed without authorisation. If a breach affects you, we'll tell you where the law requires it.

FishIn is meant for people aged 13 and over, or the higher age of digital consent in your country unless a parent or guardian consents. We don't knowingly collect data from younger children; if you think a child has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy. If a change matters, we'll show it in the app or on this page with a new date before it takes effect.

FishIn is made by an independent developer in Skopje, North Macedonia, who is the data controller. Contact: contact@fishinapp.site.
