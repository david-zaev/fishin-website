Privacy Policy

FishIn is made by an independent developer in North Macedonia. Your fishing spots are yours. This policy says what personal data FishIn handles, why, where it is kept, who receives it, how long it stays and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • No ads, no ad trackers. FishIn shows no advertising and doesn't use the advertising ID. We never sell your data or share it for advertising. The only statistics are basic, anonymous counts of installs and use (Firebase Analytics), which you can switch off in Settings.
  • You choose who sees your spots and catches: everyone, your FishIn friends, or only you. Your spot notes are always private.
  • Our server is in the EU, on Oracle Cloud in Frankfurt, Germany, and so are its encrypted backups.
  • Fish Coach chats stay on your phone. Questions are sent to Google Gemini through our server only to answer them; we don't keep them.
  • Pro is paid through Google Play. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Delete everything in the app under Settings › Account › Delete account.

Who is responsible

The data controller for FishIn is the independent developer who makes it, based in Skopje, North Macedonia ("FishIn", "we", "us"). You can reach us about anything in this policy at contact@fishinapp.site.

This policy covers the FishIn Android app (com.daev.fishin), the FishIn server at api.fishinapp.site and this website. It doesn't cover the services of other companies that FishIn uses, such as Google; their own privacy policies apply to them (see Services we use).

What stays on your phone

Much of FishIn works on the phone alone, and this data doesn't reach our server unless you share or sync it:

  • Your precise location (see Location).
  • Spot data and offline countries. The spot atlas comes with the app, and spot data you view or download is cached on the phone so the map works without signal.
  • Fish Coach chats and memory. Your conversations, and the short notes Fish Coach remembers about how you fish, are stored on the phone.
  • Rigs and their AI reviews, fish checks and forecasts you've opened.
  • Guest "About your fishing" answers. For guests these stay on the phone; once you sign in they are saved with your account.
  • Photos you don't back up. Without Pro photo backup, your catch and lure photos stay on the phone.

Uninstalling FishIn removes this data from the phone. If Android backup is switched on, Android may copy the app's data (but not your sign-in session) to your own Google account and restore it to a new phone. That backup is run by Google under your Google account settings, not by us; we can't read it.

Your account

Every install starts with a guest account: a random ID with no name or email, so you can use FishIn straight away and your log is kept safe.

You can sign in at any time, and everything you saved as a guest moves into your account:

  • With Google: we receive your Google account ID, name, email address (if Google has verified it) and a link to your Google profile photo. We don't get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • With email: we send a six-digit, short-lived sign-in code to your address. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy of the code until it is used or expires. The email is sent through Gmail.

To keep you signed in we store sign-in sessions (hashed refresh tokens with their expiry) and when you last signed in.

If you join the community you choose a public username, and can add a profile photo and short bio that other anglers see.

Location

If you allow it, FishIn uses your phone's location while the app is open to centre the map, find spots near you and show the forecast where you are. FishIn doesn't use location in the background and we don't keep a history of where you've been. FishIn works without location: you can search or move the map instead.

Some features send a place (not a record of your movements) off the phone:

  • Loading spots: the phone asks our server for the spots in the map area you are looking at.
  • Forecasts: the coordinates of a place you check go to Open-Meteo for weather, tides and river flow.
  • Fish records: the area of a spot goes to GBIF to look up which fish have been recorded there.
  • AI features: the spot you ask about and its forecast go to Google Gemini through our server.
  • Catches and spots you save include the place you choose for them.

A place you look at may be near where you are. These services receive your IP address as part of any internet connection.

Sharing and community

  • Spots you share with everyone appear on every angler's map, and are included in the spot data other phones download for offline use. Friends-only spots appear only for your FishIn friends. Shared spots show the name and pin, never your notes.
  • Catches you post show the species, size, lure, photo, your username and the name of the spot's area. They never show the exact coordinates.
  • Rigs you share show the rig, its photo and your username.
  • Your profile (username, photo, bio) can be seen by other FishIn users. Profile photos are served by a public link.
  • Leaderboard entries come only from catches you share with everyone.
  • You can remove anything you've shared, report content and block other users at any time.

Once you share something, other users can see it and could screenshot or copy it. When you delete it, it leaves our server and the shared map within minutes, but copies already on other phones, for example in an offline country download, may remain until those phones refresh their data.

Photos

  • Profile photos are stored on our server and anyone with the link can view them.
  • Photos in posts and shared rigs are stored on our server and shown only through short-lived signed links to people allowed to see the post.
  • Pro photo backup keeps compressed copies of your catch and lure photos on our server. Only you can read them.
  • Photos you send to AI features are passed to Google Gemini and not stored on our server (see Fish Coach and AI).

Photos can contain information you didn't intend to share, such as faces, number plates or landmarks. Please check a photo before you post it.

Fish Coach and AI

Fish Coach, AI fishing briefs, lure picks, fish checks, rig design and review, the lure scanner and fish photo identification are FishIn Pro features. They run on Google Gemini, always through our own server, which holds the key and builds the request.

Depending on the feature, a request may include your question, photos you send, the spot and its forecast, the fish recorded there, your tackle box, your rig, your experience level and boat or shore answer, recent messages from that chat, the notes Fish Coach remembers about you, and your app language.

  • We don't keep your questions, answers or AI photos on our server, and we don't use your data to train AI models.
  • We count each account's AI use against a monthly allowance, with short hourly and daily limits on a few heavier tasks, to keep usage fair. Only the counts are stored.
  • Google processes these requests as our service provider under the Gemini API Additional Terms. On the paid Gemini API service Google doesn't use the content to improve its products, but may keep it for a limited time to detect abuse and meet legal obligations.
  • AI answers are generated automatically and can be wrong. No decision with legal or similarly significant effects on you is made by automated means.

Please don't put personal information about yourself or others in photos or questions you send to AI features.

Purchases

FishIn Pro is sold only through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details under its own terms and privacy policy; we never see your card number, billing address or bank details.

When you buy Pro, the app sends us the purchase token Google Play gives it. Our server checks it with Google Play to switch Pro on for your account, and Google Play notifies our server of renewals, cancellations, refunds and expiry so your Pro status stays correct.

Usage statistics

FishIn uses Firebase Analytics, a service of Google, to count how many people install and use the app, so we can see what to improve. In Europe (EU/EEA/UK) it stays off unless you allow it when asked; elsewhere it is on by default, and you can switch it on or off at any time in Settings › Usage statistics.

  • What it collects: an app-instance identifier generated by the app (not your account, name or email), app version, Android version, device model, language, country or region derived from your IP address, and standard events such as first open, app opens, session length and screens viewed.
  • What it doesn't collect: your catches, spots, photos, messages, precise location or account details. FishIn doesn't send custom events about them.
  • Advertising: FishIn doesn't read the Android advertising ID, and we have turned off ad personalization, ad storage and sharing of this data for advertising.
  • Who receives it: Google, as our processor, under the Firebase data processing terms. It may be processed outside the EU/EEA (see Where data is processed).
  • How long: Google Analytics keeps event-level data for up to 14 months, set in our Firebase project, and we only look at totals.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in understanding how FishIn is used, and where the law requires it (for example in the EU/EEA and UK) your consent. You can withdraw it with the Settings switch.

Server logs and security limits

Like any web service, our server keeps short technical logs (IP address, time, request path, response and app version) to keep FishIn secure and working. Secrets and signed links are removed from them. Logs are rotated automatically and overwritten after a short time, usually days.

To stop abuse, the server counts requests per IP address and per session for a short time in memory, and limits how often sign-in codes can be sent to an address.

Phone permissions

  • Location (approximate and precise): to show spots and forecasts near you, only while you use the app. Optional.
  • Camera: to photograph catches, lures and fish. Used only when you take a photo. Optional.
  • Photos: only the pictures you pick are read, through Android's photo picker.
  • Internet and network state: to load maps, forecasts, sync and the community, and to know when you're offline.

You can change these at any time in Android's settings.

How long we keep data

  • Your account and its data are kept until you delete them or the account.
  • Guest accounts not opened for 90 days are deleted automatically with everything in them, unless they ever had Pro.
  • Pro photo backups are kept for 90 days after Pro ends, so you can renew or take them with you, and then deleted.
  • Sign-in codes are deleted when used or expired; expired sessions are deleted daily.
  • Deleting your account immediately removes your profile, username, email, catch log, tackle box, rigs, spots, reviews, posts, comments, likes, friends, reports you made and photos from our live server. Deletion can't be undone.
  • Backups: encrypted backups of the database (nightly) and photos (weekly) are kept for up to 60 days and then deleted. We don't restore deleted accounts from them.
  • Legal holds: we may keep limited data longer where the law requires it, or to deal with abuse reports, fraud or legal claims. Reports other people made about your content may be kept for this reason.

See how to delete your account. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

Legal bases

Under the EU and UK GDPR (and North Macedonia's Law on Personal Data Protection) we process your data on these bases:

  • To provide the service you asked for (contract): your account, sign-in, log, sync, spots, forecasts, AI features, photo backup and Pro.
  • To keep FishIn secure and fair and prevent abuse (legitimate interests): server logs, rate limits, AI allowances, backups, moderation and handling reports. Also to understand and improve how FishIn is used (anonymous usage statistics).
  • Where you choose to share or allow it (consent): location access, camera, spots, catches, rigs and posts you share with others, and usage statistics where the law requires consent. You can withdraw consent at any time in Android's settings or by deleting what you shared; this doesn't affect what happened before.
  • To meet legal obligations (legal obligation): for example answering lawful requests and keeping records the law requires.

Giving us data is voluntary, but without an account the app can't sync, and without the data a feature needs that feature can't work.

Where data is processed

Our server, database, photos and backups are in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). Google (Maps, Gemini, Sign-In, Gmail, Play and Firebase Analytics), Open-Meteo and GBIF may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where data leaves the EU/EEA, these providers rely on safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including in the EU, EEA, UK and North Macedonia, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct data that is wrong.
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Export data you gave us in a common machine-readable format (portability).
  • Restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for usage statistics with the switch in Settings.
  • Complain to a data protection authority, for example where you live or work. In North Macedonia that is the Personal Data Protection Agency (azlp.mk).

Much of this you can do yourself in the app: edit or delete catches, spots and posts, edit your profile, or delete your account. For anything else, email contact@fishinapp.site from the address on your account. We may need to confirm it's you, and reply within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests, in which case we'll tell you).

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, such as California, you have similar rights to know, delete and correct your data. We don't sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we won't discriminate against you for using your rights.

Security

All traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS. The database lets each account read only its own private records and what others chose to share with it. The keys for Gemini, Google Play and email live on the server, never in the app. Backups are encrypted before they leave the server, with a key that isn't kept on the server.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure and we can't guarantee that data will never be accessed, lost or disclosed without authorisation. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we'll tell you and the authorities where the law requires it.

Children

FishIn is not directed at children under 13. You must be at least 13 to use FishIn, or older where the age of digital consent in your country is higher (up to 16 in some EU countries) unless a parent or guardian has consented. We don't knowingly collect data from children below these ages. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

This website

fishinapp.site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address and browser details to serve the pages, under GitHub's privacy statement. The site loads its font from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. The website has no analytics, ads or tracking cookies (the app's usage statistics are separate, see above); it only remembers your light or dark theme choice in your own browser.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when FishIn or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change matters, we'll tell you in the app or on this page before it takes effect.

Questions, requests or complaints: contact@fishinapp.site. FishIn is made by an independent developer in Skopje, North Macedonia, who is the data controller.
