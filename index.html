The problem

Most trips are decided before you cast.

Where you stand, when you go and what you tie on matter more than luck. FishIn answers all three before you leave the house.

01

Where to go

Good water hides behind private roads and forum threads. FishIn puts piers, ramps and places to fish on one map, each with its own score.

02

When to go

Weather apps don't know fish. FishIn reads pressure, the moon, sunrise and tide into a single Fish Score for every hour.

03

What to throw

A tackle box full of lures and no plan. Fish Coach picks the lure, colour and length for today's water from what you own.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Ask Fish Coach, or email contact@fishinapp.site.

Is FishIn free?

Yes. Maps, spots, the 2-day forecast, your catch log, tackle box and friends are free. FishIn Pro adds the AI features, a 10-day forecast, photo backup and offline countries.

Does it work without signal?

Spots you've already seen stay on your phone, so the map keeps working at the water. With Pro you can save every fishing spot of a whole country before a trip.

How is the bite forecast worked out?

It combines weather and air pressure with the moon's position, moonrise and moonset, sunrise and sunset, and tides or river flow where they apply. Each hour gets a Fish Score, and you can tap a bar to see what pushes it up or down.

Do I need an account?

No. Start as a guest. Sign in with Google or an emailed code later and everything you saved comes with you, on every phone you sign in to. Pro needs an account so you can get it back on a new phone.

Are my secret spots safe?

You choose who sees each spot: everyone, only your FishIn friends, or just you. Your notes on a spot are always private, and shared catches never show exact coordinates.

Is there an iPhone version?

FishIn is on Android today, through Google Play.

How do I cancel Pro or delete my account?

Cancel Pro anytime in Google Play › Subscriptions; it stays on until the end of the period you paid for. To delete your account, open Settings › Account › Delete account in the app, or see how to delete your account.
