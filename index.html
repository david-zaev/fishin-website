Everything Built Inside FishIn

A deep, comprehensive look into the features powering every cast, spot, and catch.

AI Tactical Bite Forecast & Fish Score

Unlike generic weather forecasts, FishIn runs a multi-variable scoring model that synthesizes 3-hour barometric delta, solunar major/minor feeding peaks, solar elevation, lunar illumination, water temperature curves, and cloud cover into a unified 0-100 bite index. Delivers precise hour-by-hour tactical game plans.

  • Solunar Major & Minor feeding windows mapped to local coordinates
  • Barometric 3-hour pressure trend weighting calibrated per species
  • Generates customized tactical advice based on your actual tackle box inventory

Multimodal Tackle Scanner

Snap a photo of any lure, fly, or soft plastic. FishIn's computer vision identifies lure category, diving depth, color contrast, and suggests exact water clarity conditions for maximum hookups.

  • Identifies 12+ lure classifications from crankbaits to jigs
  • Suggests target species and optimal retrieval cadences
  • Stores lures in your digital tackle box for AI game planning

AI Fish Scanner & Biometrics

Instant camera identification of freshwater and saltwater fish. Estimates specimen length, approximate weight range, and automatically populates catch log metadata.

  • Species taxonomy identification with conservation alerts
  • Visual health and spawning condition assessment
  • Auto-fills catch log species, timestamp, and GPS coordinates

Adaptive AI Fishing Coach

Conversational domain coach adapted to the angler's skill level. Beginners receive step-by-step knot guides and safe bank spots; advanced anglers get micro-tuning on lure presentation and thermocline depth.

  • Strict domain guardrails: no hallucinated advice or drift
  • Context-aware: factors in current spot conditions and weather
  • 20 daily interactions for quick tackle tweaks on the water

Rig Engineering & Knot Studio

Interactive terminal for designing custom rigs. Calculates exact distances between floats, shot weights, swivels, and hooks. Includes step-by-step vector guides for Palomar, Uni, Blood, and FG knots.

  • Rig design verification ensures components are balanced
  • Step-by-step illustrated knot guides with breaking-strength ratings
  • Community rig sharing and offline blueprint storage

100% Offline Global Spot Atlas

Fishing happens beyond cellular towers. FishIn bundles compact spatial binary blocks (`world.bin`) allowing phones to query spots, depth boundaries, and country packs completely offline.

  • Ultra-compact binary tile format for fast zero-lag map zooms
  • Country packs downloadable for remote expeditions
  • Local GPS positioning without consuming cellular data

Automated Catch Logbook

Log catches in seconds. Automatically captures telemetry: exact weather, barometric pressure, solunar period, and lure used at the exact moment of the catch. Private by default.

  • Private by design: spots are never leaked without consent
  • High-resolution photo backup to server with FishIn Pro
  • Personal catch statistics, weight trends, and seasonal heatmaps

Community Spots & Friends

Connect with local anglers, share public catches, review community spots, and climb verified leaderboards without burning your private honeyholes.

  • Community spot updates synchronized every 5 minutes
  • Spot ratings, hazard warnings, and recent activity reports
  • Granular privacy settings: choose what to share with friends

Natural ecosystems are inherently chaotic. Claude 5.5 Haiku provides unmatched reasoning over multi-variable physical systems (barometric gradients, thermal stratification, lunar harmonics) with strict structured JSON schema adherence, ultra-fast sub-second responses, and edge efficiency.
