Delete Your FishIn Account

You hold unconditional rights to erase your telemetry and personal records. Choose between instant self-service deletion directly inside the app, or submit an email deletion request if you have already uninstalled.

IMMEDIATE IN-APP PURGE • REMOTE PROCESS WITHIN 30 DAYS • FULL ERASURE

Step-by-Step App Procedure

EXECUTES IMMEDIATELY

Execute these four taps within the FishIn Android app. All cloud database rows associated with your user ID are purged in real-time.

STEP 01
Open App
Launch FishIn on your Android device while connected to internet.
STEP 02
Settings Tab
Navigate to the bottom nav bar and tap Profile / Settings.
STEP 03
Account Submenu
Select Account preferences to manage your credentials.
STEP 04
Delete Account
Tap "Delete account" and confirm. Purge executes immediately.
Instant Execution: Once confirmed in-app, your remote data record is wiped from Supabase Frankfurt immediately. There is no grace-period recovery.

Remote Manual Deletion Request

SLA ≤ 30 CALENDAR DAYS

If you have uninstalled the app or lost your mobile device, submit an authenticated deletion request via email.

Mandatory Authentication Instructions:

To prevent unauthorized account destruction, the request must be dispatched from the exact Google Account email address linked to your FishIn profile.

To: Developer email shown on FishIn's official Google Play Store listing
Subject: FishIn Account Deletion Request - [Your Username / Email]
Body: "Please permanently delete my FishIn account and all associated catch logs, tackle box entries, and cloud photo backups."

What is Permanently Erased

  • ✓
    Angler Profile: Username, profile biography, avatar image, and verified Google link.
  • ✓
    Catch Logbook: All historical catches, timestamps, species telemetry, and personal catch notes.
  • ✓
    Digital Tackle Box: Cataloged lures, rigs, tackle specs, and custom gear configurations.
  • ✓
    Pro Cloud Photo Vault: All backed up catch and lure images stored on our Frankfurt cloud storage.
  • ✓
    Community Footprint: Shared spot pins, catch feed posts, reviews, likes, and comment threads.
  • ✓
    Social Graph: Friends list, pending invitations, blocks, and reports.
  • ✓
    AI Telemetry Record: Quota usage counts, purchase validation tokens, and API records.

What is Not Affected

  • —
    On-Phone Offline Waypoints: Private spots saved strictly in phone memory stay on device storage until you manually uninstall the app.
  • !
    Google Play Subscription: Subscriptions are billed by Google Play. Deleting your FishIn account does NOT cancel Google Play billing. You must cancel active renewals in the Google Play Store under Payments & Subscriptions.
