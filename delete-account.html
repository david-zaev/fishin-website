Account

Delete your account

You can delete your FishIn account and its data at any time. In the app it happens straight away.

FishIn: Fishing Log & Spotscom.daev.fishin
Instant

In the app

  1. Open FishIn.
  2. Open Settings (the profile button at the top of the map).
  3. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  4. Confirm.
Within 30 days

By email

No longer have the app? Email contact@fishinapp.site from the address you sign in to FishIn with, with the subject "Delete FishIn Account".

We may ask you to confirm the request from that address. Confirmed requests are completed within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile, username and email address, catch log, tackle box, catch and lure photos, shared spots, reviews, posts, comments, friends and "About your fishing" answers are deleted for good, from your phone too. Deletion can't be undone.

Spot data saved on the phone for offline use stays until you uninstall the app.

Kept for a limited time

Copies in server backups are overwritten within 30 days. Limited records may be kept longer only where the law requires it, or to handle abuse reports or legal claims.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel FishIn Pro and doesn't refund the remaining time. Google Play bills the subscription, so cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions before you delete your account.

Guest accounts

Never signed in? Guest accounts that aren't opened for 90 days are deleted automatically with everything in them. Uninstalling the app removes everything on the phone straight away.
