ACCOUNT DELETION & PRIVACY

Delete Account & Data

You can permanently delete your FishIn account and all associated personal records at any time.

Option 1: In the App (Instant)

To delete your account immediately inside the FishIn Android app:

  1. Open the FishIn app on your phone.
  2. Tap the Profile / Settings tab in the bottom bar.
  3. Tap Account preferences.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.
Your account credentials, catch logs, and tackle box entries are wiped from the server database immediately.

Option 2: By Email (If App Uninstalled)

If you no longer have the app installed, send an email from the Google account linked to your FishIn profile to the developer contact email listed on our Google Play page with the subject "Delete FishIn Account".

View Developer Email on Google Play
Manual email requests are verified and permanently purged within 30 days.

Data Handled

Permanently Deleted: Profile name, email link, logged catches, catch photos, tackle box records, and shared spots.

Google Play Subscriptions: Subscriptions are billed by Google Play. Deleting your account does not cancel recurring Google Play billing. Please cancel any active renewals directly in Google Play under Payments & Subscriptions.
