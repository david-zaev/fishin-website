Fishing safely and legally

  • Follow the law. You're responsible for having any fishing licence or permit you need and for following the rules where you fish, including closed seasons, protected species, minimum sizes, bag limits, gear and bait restrictions, catch-and-release rules, night-fishing bans, protected areas and boating rules. Rules change and differ by country, region and water. FishIn doesn't tell you them and you must check them with the local authority.
  • A pin is not permission. A spot on the map doesn't mean it exists, is open, is public or may be fished. Check access yourself, respect private land, fences, signs, closures and the people who live and work there.
  • Water is dangerous. Drowning, cold water, currents, tides, waves, flooding, rising rivers, slippery rocks, soft banks, ice, lightning, heat and darkness can kill. Check the official weather, water and ice conditions yourself, wear a life jacket where appropriate, never fish alone in risky places, and tell someone where you're going.
  • Gear and handling. Hooks, knives, lines, rods near power lines, boats, engines and fish spines and teeth can cause injury. Knot, rig and gear advice in FishIn is general guidance; check your gear and its strength yourself.
  • Eating fish. FishIn doesn't tell you whether a fish is safe to eat. Some waters carry consumption advisories or pollution; check local advice.
  • Not for emergencies. FishIn is not a navigation or emergency service. In an emergency call your local emergency number (112 in Europe).
  • You fish at your own risk. Fishing, boating, wading and travelling to spots carry a risk of injury, death, illness and loss of or damage to property. You take part voluntarily and accept these risks.

Spots, forecasts and AI

  • Spots aren't checked. Spots are generated automatically from public map data or added by other anglers. They may be inaccurate, outdated, unsafe, closed, private or illegal to fish. We don't inspect, verify or endorse any location.
  • Forecasts are estimates. Fish Scores, bite times, weather, pressure, tides, river flow and fish records come from models and third-party data and can be wrong, late or missing.
  • AI can be wrong. Fish Coach, briefs, lure picks, fish checks, rig designs and reviews, the lure scanner and fish identification are produced automatically by Google Gemini. They may be inaccurate, incomplete or made up, even when they sound sure. Check anything important yourself.
  • None of this is navigation, safety, medical, food-safety, legal or regulatory advice. Never rely on FishIn to decide whether it's safe to go out, whether a fish is protected or legal to keep, or whether it's safe to eat.
  • No guaranteed catch. Nothing in FishIn promises you'll catch fish, and a slow day isn't a fault in the service.
  • AI answers are for your personal use. Don't present them as professional advice to others.
