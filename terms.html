Fishing safely and legally

  • Follow the law. You're responsible for having a valid fishing licence and following closed seasons, size and bag limits and protection rules where you fish.
  • A pin is not permission. A spot on the map doesn't mean you may go there. Check access, respect private land and obey signs and local rules.
  • Water is dangerous. Check currents, tides, weather and ice yourself. Never rely on FishIn for your safety.
  • You fish at your own risk. Fishing, boating, wading and travelling to spots carry a risk of injury, death, illness and loss of or damage to property. You take part voluntarily and at your own risk.

  • No guaranteed catch. Scores, forecasts and AI advice don't promise you'll catch fish, and a slow day isn't a fault in the service.
  • Never rely on FishIn to decide whether a fish is protected, legal to keep or safe to eat.
