How to tie the FG knot

Braid woven round the leader in crossing turns, locked with half hitches. Slim enough to run through the rod rings.

Step by step

  1. Hold the braid tight and lay the leader across it.
  2. Wrap the braid round the leader in criss-cross turns, front then back, 15 to 20 times.
  3. Lock it with a few half hitches round both lines.
  4. Wet the knot, pull it tight slowly and trim the end.

When to use it

Best pickBraid to leader: the FG knot is slim and strong, and runs through the rod rings when you cast.
