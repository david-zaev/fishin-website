How to tie the Dropper loop knot

A loop twisted out of the middle of the line, standing off it, to hang a hook from.

Step by step

  1. Make a loop in the line where you want the dropper.
  2. Twist the loop's two legs round each other five or six times.
  3. Push the loop down through the middle twist and pull it out below.
  4. Wet the knot, pull it tight slowly and trim the end.

When to use it

Best pickA dropper loop stands out from the main line, so the side hook doesn't tangle round it.
