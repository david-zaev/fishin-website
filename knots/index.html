Knot guides

16 fishing knots, step by step.

Every knot FishIn's rig builder uses: what it's for, which line it suits, how strong it is and how to tie it. In the app, each one is tied in 3D.

Hooks, swivels and lures

Line to an eye or ring: hooks, swivels, snaps and lures.

Improved clinch

The classic: five to seven turns of the tag end round the line, back through the first loop, then through the big loop it makes.

Strength ~90%EasyMono

Palomar

The line doubled through the eye, an overhand knot tied with the loop, and the loop passed over the hook or lure.

Strength ~95%EasyMono, Fluorocarbon, Braid

Uni knot

Turns made inside a loop laid along the line, then pulled down to the eye. Works in any line.

Strength ~90%EasyMono, Fluorocarbon, Braid

San Diego jam

Turns over both strands, the tag back through the bottom loop and the top one. Very strong in fluorocarbon.

Strength ~95%MediumMono, Fluorocarbon

Snell

Turns round the hook's shank instead of the eye, so the pull comes straight in line with the point.

Strength ~95%MediumMono, Fluorocarbon

Rapala loop

A non-slip loop at the lure's eye, so the lure swings freely and swims as it's meant to.

Strength ~90%MediumMono, Fluorocarbon

Crimp sleeve

A metal sleeve pressed shut with crimping pliers. For wire, which doesn't hold a knot.

Strength ~95%EasyWire

Spade end knot

Turns whipped round the shank just under the spade, for hooks without an eye. The spade stops it sliding off.

Strength ~95%Takes practiceMono, Fluorocarbon

Line to line

Joining two lines: main line to leader, or mending a break.

Double uni

A uni knot in each line round the other, pulled together. Joins almost any two lines.

Strength ~85%EasyMono, Fluorocarbon, Braid

Blood knot

The two ends wrapped round each other and tucked back through the middle. Neat and slim.

Strength ~85%MediumMono, Fluorocarbon

Surgeon's knot

Both lines laid together and tied in a double overhand. Quick, even with cold hands.

Strength ~85%EasyMono, Fluorocarbon

FG knot

Braid woven round the leader in crossing turns, locked with half hitches. Slim enough to run through the rod rings.

Strength ~95%Takes practiceBraid, Mono, Fluorocarbon

Albright

The thin line wrapped round a loop of the thick one. Good for lines of very different thickness.

Strength ~85%MediumBraid, Mono, Fluorocarbon, Wire

Loops

Loops for droppers and loop-to-loop connections.

Dropper loop

A loop twisted out of the middle of the line, standing off it, to hang a hook from.

Strength ~80%MediumMono, Fluorocarbon

Perfection loop

A neat loop at the end of a line, for joining loop to loop.

Strength ~90%MediumMono, Fluorocarbon

Stops

A stop on the line to set the depth of a sliding float.

Stop knot

A few turns of thread or line tied round the main line. It slides to set the depth and still runs through the rings.

Strength ~100%EasyMono, Fluorocarbon, Braid
